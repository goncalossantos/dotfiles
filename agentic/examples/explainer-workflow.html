Start with the diagram. Make video optional.

A good explanation builds a mental model first. HTML lets the reader inspect that model at their own pace. Video adds timing and narration when those help.

Example: To teach a cache, draw “request → cache → database.” Then show the different paths for a cache hit and a cache miss. A moving presenter is not needed to explain that relationship.
